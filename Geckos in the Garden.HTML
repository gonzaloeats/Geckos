The Artist Journey

How It All Began

A small, forlorn, coffee-stained side table jumpstarted my journey into refurbishing and painting furniture. Rather than restore the table to its original color scheme of forest green and brown, I decided to paint it white and see where a brush dipped in black would take me. As leaves and vines crawled up the legs and flowers bloomed across the top, I immersed myself in peaceful contemplation.
